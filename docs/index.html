    05Room V · Principles

    How we behave.

    1. Own for the long term.No sale date, no fund life, no forced exit.
    2. Leave management in charge.The people who built it keep running it.
    3. Fund growth from earnings.Modest leverage, reinvested cash.
    4. Say the hard thing early.Bad news travels up the same day.
